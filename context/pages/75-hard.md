# 75 Hard Page (Amol only)

← [Hub: skills.md](../skills.md) · Status: **Defined**

**Purpose:** a simple daily checklist of the 75 Hard rules. Only visible on Amol's profile. Kept as simple as possible for now.

## Daily checklist (standard 75 Hard rules)

- [ ] Follow the diet: no cheat meals, no alcohol
- [ ] Workout 1: 45 minutes
- [ ] Workout 2: 45 minutes, outdoors
- [ ] Drink 1 gallon (about 3.8 L) of water
- [ ] Read 10 pages of a non-fiction book
- [ ] Take a progress photo

## Behaviour

- **Day counter:** "Day X of 75". On first use, the page asks "What day are you on?" so a challenge already in progress can be picked up; it then counts up automatically.
- **Missed item = restart:** if any item isn't ticked by midnight, the counter resets to Day 1.
- **Kept simple:** no history grid and no reminders; just today's checklist and the day counter.
- The workouts aren't planned here; this page only ticks them off.
